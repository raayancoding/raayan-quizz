RaayanQuizz

Version 1.2.0

A playful, offline-friendly quiz app made for/by Raayan — turn any lesson into a fun, interactive game.

✨ What this app can do

  • 📚 Browse quizzes organised by grade → subject → chapter (Grade 4 and NCERT Grade 8).
  • 📖 Flipbook reader for every chapter — read aloud, zoom, then “Quiz me on this page”.
  • 🎮 Buddy, themes, XP levels, daily streak and sticker mystery boxes.
  • 🩹 Mistake bank with spaced repetition, ⚡ 60-second Challenge mode.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent view (long-press the logo) with export/import of progress.
  • 📱 Installable app that works offline for anything you've opened.
  • 🎚️ Pick how many questions, the difficulty, a topic, timer and shuffle.
  • 💡 Instant explanations after each answer, with a friendly mascot.
  • ⏸️ Pause an “All questions” quiz and continue later from the same question.
  • 🏅 Earn trophies & stamps for correct answers and finished quizzes.
